# Changelog

## 3.000 - 2026-10-17

First formal Zhongdou Sans release.

- Center Simplified Chinese and default-language punctuation horizontally and vertically.
- Default curly quotes to half-width forms and add the centered U+22EF ellipsis.
- Center brackets and corner quotes across the complete variable-weight range.
- Align horizontal metrics with Sarasa Fixed SC for Pango and GTK font fallback.
- Remove unrelated alphanumerics, symbols, and inherited OpenType features.
- Provide a reproducible FontTools build, specimens, and a project website.
